# Credit reporting and identity-dispute policy
Customers disputing information on a credit report should identify the disputed entry and explain why it appears inaccurate. Support staff should record the dispute and route it to an authorised reviewer. The AI assistant must not state that a credit record will be deleted or altered. Suspected identity theft or account takeover requires prompt human escalation and approved identity-verification procedures.

# Debt collection complaints policy
Complaints involving repeated contact, disputed debts, threats, harassment or collection activity must be escalated to trained staff. Customers may be asked for a case or account reference through approved secure channels, but the support interface must not request passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes or full bank/card numbers. The AI assistant must not make legal conclusions about whether a debt is valid or enforceable.

# Mortgage and secured-lending policy
Mortgage complaints involving payments, servicing, escrow, arrears, foreclosure or repossession require human review because they may materially affect a customer's home or finances. The AI assistant may explain that the case has been prioritised but must not promise payment changes, pauses, refunds, interest adjustments or legal outcomes.

# Credit card policy
Customers reporting duplicate charges, unauthorised transactions, card access problems, fees or billing disputes should provide a brief description and non-sensitive transaction reference information. Full card numbers, PINs, CVVs and one-time passcodes must never be requested in free text. Fraud or suspected account compromise should receive elevated priority and human review.

# Bank account policy
Complaints concerning checking/savings accounts, deposits, withdrawals, fees, account access or missing funds should be recorded with the date and a concise description. High-impact access issues or suspected unauthorised activity should be escalated. Staff confirm any account adjustment or reimbursement; AI-generated drafts must not promise an outcome.

# Loans policy
Student, vehicle, personal, payday and other loan complaints may involve repayment, servicing, interest, affordability or account administration. The assistant may provide process guidance but must not provide personalised financial advice, change repayment terms or state that a customer qualifies for a particular remedy. Material decisions remain with authorised staff.

# Payments, transfers and money-services policy
Complaints involving transfers, prepaid products, virtual currency or money services should capture the non-sensitive transaction reference, approximate date and issue description. Missing-money, fraud and unauthorised-transfer cases should be prioritised. The assistant must not guarantee reversal, recovery or completion dates.

# Customer communication and human oversight
AI-generated responses are drafts only. A human agent remains accountable for final communications, exceptional cases, policy interpretation and decisions that could materially affect rights, finances or access to services. Responses should be respectful, concise, transparent about uncertainty and grounded in the approved policy knowledge base.

# Data minimisation, privacy and retention
Only information necessary to resolve the support request should be collected. Customers must not submit passwords, PINs, one-time passcodes, full payment-card numbers, full bank-account numbers or unrelated sensitive information. Public research datasets used for model development must be handled according to their source terms and documented limitations. Demonstration exports should avoid re-identifying individuals or combining fields in ways that increase privacy risk.
