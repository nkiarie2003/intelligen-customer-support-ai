# Bitext Intent Classifier Setup

## Purpose

This feature supplements the broad CFPB product classifier with a
fine-grained customer-support intent prediction.

Example:

- Product classification: Payments Transfers
- Customer intent: Track Refund

## Dataset

Download the Bitext Customer Support dataset:

https://huggingface.co/datasets/bitext/Bitext-customer-support-llm-chatbot-training-dataset
